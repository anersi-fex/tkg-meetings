“Save this number as James, the insurance guy.” (or your own name)

Layout: Script, the words to say: you in coral, the client in the lighter rows. The full script: “Yeah, okay. So it was sent to underwriting, which is totally normal. That happens a lot. It just means there’s something in your file history that’s coming up as a flag. That’s fine. It happens a lot. Do you have any idea what that might be?” [let them talk] “They’re going to want me to call them tomorrow morning and sort of explain. So I’m happy to go to bat for you. I won’t tell them more than they need to know. But I’m the one who needs to know, so I know what to keep from them.” [they tell you] “Awesome, cool. Okay, I’ll clear it up tomorrow morning. As soon as I get off the phone with them, I’m going to give you a call.” [they say okay] “Thank you very much, sir. I’ll be in touch shortly. Save this number as James, the insurance guy. I don’t want it to get missed or go to spam and not even ring. Sometimes it doesn’t even ring. Just save this phone number, or at least remember the area code. I’ll give you a call tomorrow, okay?” [they say okay] “All right. Have a great evening.” Then hang up.
