# CLAUDE.md — TKG Meetings

**Owner:** Anersi Koka (The Koka Group, Troy MI). Licensed MI P&C and life producer at Goosehead; runs a life-insurance phone sales team. Not a developer: short replies, verdict first, one step at a time, a picture when possible, receipts before "done". He reads on his iPhone.

## What this repo is
Team meeting material for The Koka Group: talking points (`meetings/`), the slide brand (`brand/`), and the **Meetings app** (the Next.js app at the repo root; plan in `design/meetings-app/PROPOSAL.md`, approved and built 2026-11-14: "yes please, build it now!"). Pocket voice notes filed by Command Center into its Team meeting folder become slides in the approved brand; the owner checks them on his phone, plays them on the office TV and clicks through from the phone. Setup: `SETUP.md`. State and backlog: `HANDOFF.md`.

## Slides: the rule
- **Every team meeting slide follows `brand/SLIDES.md`** (approved by the owner on 2026-11-04: "I actually love all these slides... move forward with this"). Pick one of its 12 layouts, start from that layout's template in `brand/slides/`, keep its colors, type sizes, frame and footer, and stay inside its limits. Do not invent new layouts, colors or sizes without the owner's OK.
- The reference deck is https://claude.ai/artifact/7ic1ijVaJ97r6U1cScfWhG. The picture of all 12 layouts is `brand/slides/contact-sheet.png` (`node scripts/render-slides.mjs` re-renders it).
- Slides carry the owner's words, shortened; speaker notes carry the rest. Never invent a number, percentage, date, name, quote or promise: an open item wears the amber TBD pill and a `[X]` placeholder. Numbers about sales come from Scorecard (Supabase project `tkg-scorecard`, read only).

## The app
- Stack (matches Scorecard; do not substitute): Next.js 16.3.4 App Router, TypeScript strict, Tailwind 4, React 19 with the React Compiler lint rules, `src/proxy.ts` (not middleware), async `params`/`searchParams`/`cookies()`. Read `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` before a new convention. A `"use server"` file exports only async functions.
- Screens: Pocket `/pocket` (his recordings of the last 7 days with Pocket's own summary; Make a meeting adds one to the next meeting as a thought `pocket:<id>` with the transcript for drafting only, stripped by `forBrowser` before any client component; one the TV never reached carries into the next meeting (`carryPocketThoughts`); Command Center's card for the same recording links to it as `card.cc_id` (`sameMoment`) and is marked done in its place; Whole meeting (owner, 2026-11-14) makes one recording the whole meeting: `planWholeMeeting` in draft.ts splits it into parts at Brief / Standard / Full (`WHOLE_LENGTHS`, `fitPlan` in pocket-shape.ts), each part a thought `pocket:<id>#n` with `card.whole` drafted with its planned slide count (up to 3), the other thoughts switched off by the app (`switchOffOthers`; they come back next meeting); Next meeting's whole-meeting card (`whole-card.tsx`) changes the length or takes a "Tell Claude" and makes it again (`makeWholeMeeting` in `src/lib/whole.ts`, parts replaced); each thought's editor has Ask Claude (`askClaudeItem`: `card.ask` + `card.revise`, the current slides, read by `userMessage`); Skip hides a recording (`decision` 'skipped', migration 0003); Command Center only records the choice; `mtg_pocket`, migrations 0002 and 0003; `src/lib/pocket.ts`, `pocket-shape.ts`; key in Settings or `POCKET_API_KEY`), Next meeting `/` (the thoughts, switches, order, cover, Scorecard slide, closing quote, Play), one thought `/item/[id]` (live preview, layout picker, fields with limits, notes, Redo with Claude), the clicker `/present`, Preview `/deck`, Past `/past`, Settings `/settings` (Command Center connector, Claude key, the TV address), the 12 layouts `/layouts`, the TV `/tv` (polls `GET /api/show?k=<meetingId:deckVersion>` every second; the deck comes only when that key changed, so a TV left open for weeks never shows last week's slides; speaker notes only ever go to the owner's role; reloads itself when the build changes; F full screen, wake lock, the cursor hides). Pause keeps the slide and Play resumes on the same thought ("Resume on the TV"; "Start from the cover" restarts). End meeting works from the clicker or from Next meeting and marks done in Command Center only the thoughts the TV showed (`mtg_meetings.shown_items`, filled by the SQL that moves the slide); `endPreview` lists them first. End marks them done before the meeting closes. A thought seen in an earlier meeting comes back with its slides, his edits and its switch; thoughts that arrive after Play (live or paused) come in switched off with `off_by_app` and the note "Waiting for the next meeting", and come back on at the next meeting if he never touched the switch.
- Sign-in: `OWNER_PASSWORD` (everything) and `TEAM_PASSWORD` (the TV and `/api/show` only; every other page sends the team to `/tv`), constant-time check, HMAC cookie `mtg_session` (180 days, carries the role and a password tag, so changing a password signs that role out), login throttle in `mtg_login_attempts`: every try is recorded before the password is checked, in one SQL call with a per-address lock (`mtg_login_attempt`: 10 in 15 minutes per address; during a flood of 500 in 15 minutes only addresses that already tried are refused, so a fresh address still gets a try, and past 2,000 everyone waits; IPv6 counted by /64), and only an owner sign-in clears an address's count. `requirePage()` in every page, an owner check in every server action.
- Data: Meetings' own Supabase project **tkg-meetings** (`ukpqonskyakwvnstjejn`, us-east-2; owner's yes 2026-11-14, because Scorecard's database now holds client details), server only, service role key (`src/lib/supabase.ts`; every call goes through `freshFetch`, because Next memoizes identical GET fetches within one render and a read after a write would get the old answer). Meetings' tables start with `mtg_` (`supabase/migrations/0001_meetings.sql`: meetings, items, settings, login attempts, and the functions `mtg_move_slide`, `mtg_put_deck` (an atomic compare-and-set deck swap: a deck rebuilt during the show keeps the same thought on screen), `mtg_claim_draft` (claims a thought for drafting only while fewer than three of that meeting's thoughts are drafting) and `mtg_login_attempt`; RLS on, no policies, grants revoked). Scorecard is read only through two security-definer functions pasted into Scorecard's project (`supabase/scorecard/meetings_feed.sql`: `meetings_scorecard_month` = the month's life total, policies and goal exactly as Scorecard's Scoreboard counts them, P&C left out, a running month through today; `meetings_scorecard_names` = active names and life carriers), called with Scorecard's publishable key (`SCORECARD_SUPABASE_URL`, `SCORECARD_SUPABASE_KEY`, treat it as a secret anyway) or, when shared, on the same project (`src/lib/scorecard.ts`). Never read Scorecard's tables directly: they hold client details. PostgREST refuses an `or=` filter on an update: use plain filters or an rpc.
- Command Center (`src/lib/command-center.ts`): JSON-RPC `tools/call` to `/api/mcp` with the connector token; `tkg_notebook {group: "team", status: "open"}` in, `tkg_move_card {id, status: "done"}` after the meeting. Keep only cards whose `group_ids` holds `team`: Command Center returns every open card when the group is not one of his folders. The token and a Claude key may be pasted in Settings (`mtg_settings` row `secrets`, never sent to the browser); env vars win.
- Drafting (`src/lib/draft.ts`): one Claude call per thought, Claude Opus 5.5 (`MEETINGS_MODEL` overrides), `beta.messages.create` with `output_config.format` (the zod schema with const and enum kept), the server-side fallback beta, adaptive thinking at medium effort, the system prompt cached; `stop_reason` is checked first and the answer is read leniently, each slide through `cleanSlide`. Time counts from when the page or action started (`RUN_CLOCK` in `src/lib/checks.ts`): every call ends inside `maxDuration` 300 and none starts with under 120 s left; a claim goes stale after 6 minutes; at most three drafts run at once per meeting (`claimDraft(id, { max: 3 })` through `mtg_claim_draft`), and a run keeps draining the meeting's queue; a draft saves only onto the row it claimed (guarded on `updated_at`). The Claude error mapping (`claudeTrouble`) lives in `src/lib/checks.ts`. No key or a failed call: a plain Key line of his own title (`fallbackSlide`, section from `fallbackSection`), with the reason in plain words (out of credit, a model the key can't use, busy). Numbers on a slide that the card never says are flagged by value, with k and M and spoken dates (`numbersNotInCard` in `src/lib/checks.ts`), and so are capitalised names on neither the team list, the carrier list, the card's people, nor a built-in list of everyday proper nouns (Social Security, Medicare, The Koka Group, months, states, …); a word from the card clears only when the card also writes it in lower case, so a client's name he said still gets the Check badge; a closing quote is offered only when the card has it in quote marks. Logs carry ids and error classes only.
- Slides: `src/lib/slides.ts` (schemas, limits, `cleanSlide`, `switchLayout`), `src/components/slides/slide.tsx` (the 12 layouts on a 1920x1080 canvas sized by `--u`; fixed hex colors from `brand/SLIDES.md`, the one place a component may hardcode colors), `src/lib/deck.ts` (cover, scoreboard, agenda, sections, Housekeeping last, quote; at most 5 sections; red slides never two in a row and at most a third).
- The app's own look is Tide in the Crimson colorway (`src/app/globals.css`, semantic tokens only in components). Icons, the link card and the iPhone opening screens come from `npm run icons` (`scripts/gen-icons.mjs`, the speech-bubble mark of `src/components/brand.tsx`).
- Forms that can fail submit through `onSubmit` + `startTransition`, never a form `action` prop (React 19 resets the form). Every awaited server action in a client component goes through `safeAction` (`src/lib/safe-action.ts`): a lost connection returns plain words instead of crashing the page, and an action from before a deploy answers `stale`, upon which the page reloads itself (`reloadForUpdate`, at most once in 30 s; the editor keeps unsaved slides across it, `src/lib/layout-memory.ts`, which also remembers each layout he tried). `error.tsx` screens and `global-error.tsx` catch the rest. The TV and the clicker poll one request at a time and drop answers older than the newest; Next meeting refreshes once at a time while drafting.

## How to run and check
- `npm install`, `npm run dev`. Before every push: `npm run lint`, `npx tsc --noEmit` (`npx next typegen` first when routes change), `npm test`, `npm run build`.
- End to end on a fresh database: `scripts/local-db/start.sh --reset` (Postgres 16 + PostgREST; applies Scorecard's tables from `scripts/local-db/scorecard/` with invented numbers, then Meetings' migrations), the stand-ins `node scripts/mocks/command-center.mjs` (:4030, token `test-cc-token`; `POST /__add` adds a card) and `node scripts/mocks/claude.mjs` (:4031), `node scripts/mocks/pocket.mjs` (:4032, key `pk_test_meetings_key_1234`), then build and start the app with `.local-db/env` plus `OWNER_PASSWORD`, `TEAM_PASSWORD`, `COMMAND_CENTER_URL=http://127.0.0.1:4030/api/mcp`, `COMMAND_CENTER_TOKEN=test-cc-token`, `ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL=http://127.0.0.1:4031`, `POCKET_API_BASE=http://127.0.0.1:4032/api/v1/public` and no `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` or `POCKET_API_KEY`, then `node scripts/smoke.mjs` (screenshots in `test-results/`). Kill the app by PID or exact process name: a `pkill -f` pattern that appears in your own shell command kills that shell.
- Migrations: `supabase/migrations/` go into Meetings' project, `supabase/scorecard/meetings_feed.sql` into Scorecard's (`tkg-scorecard`, `fkdjinmatdkmpsboyyit`); the owner pastes them or, with his yes, the session applies them through the Supabase connector. Applied by the session at his yes: `0001_meetings` and `0002_pocket` (2026-11-14) and `0003_pocket_skip` (2026-11-15) on tkg-meetings and `meetings_feed` on tkg-scorecard (checked right after: RLS on every `mtg_` table, no grants for anon or authenticated, functions for service_role only; the feed returned November's life total and the API roles still cannot read `sales`). Anything that needs a new migration merges only after it ran.
