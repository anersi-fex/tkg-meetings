## 1. Comp increase and the 90-day rule

- **If you just got here,** there's a 90-day grace period on the comp increase I'm giving. You need to be here in the office 90 working days first.
- **90 means** 90 working days. Logged in and in the office. Not 90 days from the day you were hired.
- **Promotion:** it's yours. It's [X%] on commission. It just starts paying after your 90th working day.
- **When it kicks in:** the next day after your 90th working day. Active from then on.

## 7. If nobody's free: the underwriting rule
- **Only after** you've tried everyone in section 6 and everyone is busy.
- **Tell the client** you've submitted everything to underwriting. You have to wait and see what you hear back.
- **Set the wait:** it shouldn't take too long. You'll be back with a decision in just a bit, probably tomorrow.
- **What underwriting tells you:** whether it's worth it for them to apply at all. If so, you walk them through it.
- **Have them save your number** as "James, the insurance guy" (or your own name), so the callback doesn't get missed or flagged as spam.

## 8. The underwriting script, word for word
Say it like this. The client's side is in brackets.

> "Yeah, okay. So it was sent to underwriting, which is totally normal. That happens a lot."
>
> "It just means there's something in your file history that's coming up as a flag. That's fine. It happens a lot."
>
> "Do you have any idea what that might be?"
>
> [let them talk]
>
> "They're going to want me to call them tomorrow morning and sort of explain."
>
> "So I'm happy to go to bat for you. I won't tell them more than they need to know."
>
> "But I'm the one who needs to know, so I know what to keep from them."
>
> [they tell you]
>
> "Awesome, cool. Okay, I'll clear it up tomorrow morning."
>
> "As soon as I get off the phone with them, I'm going to give you a call."
>
> [they say okay]
>
> "Thank you very much, sir. I'll be in touch shortly."
>
> "Save this number as James, the insurance guy." (or your own name)
>
> "I don't want it to get missed or go to spam and not even ring. Sometimes it doesn't even ring."
>
> "Just save this phone number, or at least remember the area code."
>
> "I'll give you a call tomorrow, okay?"
>
> [they say okay]
>
> "All right. Have a great evening."

Then hang up. That's the order of operations when nobody is there, everyone is busy, and you've tried everything. Do exactly this.
